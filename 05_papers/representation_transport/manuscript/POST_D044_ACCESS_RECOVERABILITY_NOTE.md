## Facts

These facts were supplied by the author after D044. They concern the restricted-use data for the Vermont Welfare Restructuring Project, ICPSR Study 38060.

- **Access mechanism:** Secure Download.
- **Eligibility:** researchers based in Sweden may apply. ICPSR institutional membership is not mandatory.
- **Cost:** for a researcher outside a member institution, ICPSR quoted a total access cost of USD 1,174.
- **Required documents:** an ethics / IRB review document, a Data Security Plan and a Restricted Data Use Agreement (RDUA).
- **Signatures:** the RDUA requires both the principal investigator and an authorised institutional representative.
- **AI / ML use:** any AI / ML use would need additional Privacy and Security approval. It is generally incompatible with ordinary Secure Download arrangements.
- **Project status:** this research is a personal, independent project.
- **Author's decision:** the author does not plan to pay the fee or pursue restricted access at present.
- **Variable content:** at the time of this update, ICPSR had not confirmed the exact variables in the restricted file.

**Not recorded:** correspondence text, email addresses, ticket identifiers or tokens.
