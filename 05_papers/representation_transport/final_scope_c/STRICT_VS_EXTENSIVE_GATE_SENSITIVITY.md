# Strict vs extensive gate sensitivity

The registered gate (D013 / D041) accepts only E-A or E-A (caveat) employment estimands. The **unregistered, secondary** extensive view also admits E-B objects, such as hours-threshold or wage-threshold employment and days employed.

Support-union family counts (not prevalence) of isolated families with usable SE / CI:

| Dimension | Strict (registered) | Extensive (secondary, NOT REGISTERED) |
|---|---|---|
| WITHDRAWAL | 1 (TF-BMINCOME) | 2 (adds LA-TF-X057-WAGENINGEN-PW) |
| CONDITIONALITY | 1 (TF-UTRECHT) | 2 (adds LA-TF-X057-WAGENINGEN-PW) |
| CADENCE | 0 | 0 |

**Under the broader E-B-inclusive view, support looks stronger.** Withdrawal and conditionality would each have an independent pair, and both pairs would run through Wageningen.

This does **not** change the registered result:

- **The trigger stays as registered.** The final H002 trigger is computed under the registered strict gate only, and is NOT MET.
- **This is a sensitivity / research-design observation.** It shows that the conclusion depends on the estimand target. It does not provide support.
- **Wageningen is not promoted into the strict gate.** Its E-B estimand stays secondary.
- **Changing the target needs a prospective decision.** Moving from P(employed) to an extensive-margin family would require a new decision, taken before any use of the result, with its own rationale. It may never be adopted because it would make a trigger pass.
